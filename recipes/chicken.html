Grilled Chicken

Juicy grilled chicken marinated with garlic, lemon, and herbs.

Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast
  • Olive Oil
  • Garlic
  • Lemon Juice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Mixed herbs

Steps

  1. Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, and herbs.
  2. Marinate the chicken for 30 minutes.
  3. Grill over medium heat for 6–8 minutes per side.
  4. Serve hot.
