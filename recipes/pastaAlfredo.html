Pasta Alfredo

This pasta is a combination of many cultures. With a deep delicous savory flavor that melts in your mouth, this recipe will sure be one of your favorites

Ingredients

  • Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • Whole Milk
  • Pasta
  • Freshly Chopped Parsely
  • Freshly Ground Black Pepper
  • Unsalted Butter

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package directions, drain, and place in a large bowl.
  2. In a saucepan, add the milk, butter, parmesan cheese and seasoning mix and stir over a medium heat until creamy.
  3. Pour the cheese mixture over the pasta and toss until well coated.
  4. Serve immediately garnished with the parsley and pepper.
